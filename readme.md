All you need is a browser. Just click and play these games without any distractions.

## Singleplayer

- [2 Minute Drill](https://2mindrillgame.org/) - `Sports` `Strategy` `Daily` - Take over a real NFL ending: call plays and manage the clock.
- [Arrow Puzzle](https://arrowpuzzle.app/) - `Puzzle` `Logic` `Casual` - Trace curved paths and clear every arrow in the right order.
- [Continuum](https://www.scalargame.com/continuum) - `Puzzle` `Estimation` `Daily` - Drag countries into place on a hidden scale like population or area.
- [DailyPlay Sudoku](https://dailyplay.com/play/sudoku) - `Puzzle` `Logic` `Daily` - Classic 9x9 Sudoku with one new grid a day, the same for everyone.
- [Erebos Arcade](https://arcade.ereboslab.com) - `Puzzle` `Casual` - Water sort, hexa sort, block puzzle and more, no signup required.
- [FTL: Faster Than Light (Demo)](https://www.humblebundle.com/play/asmjs/ftl_asm_demo/demo) - `Roguelite` `Real-Time` - Adventure in a galaxy filled with glory and bitter defeat.
- [Glimmer Haul](https://glimmer.dayplaygames.com/) - `Arcade` `Casual` `Daily` - Bank sparks through hoops in 75-second runs.
- [Gravity Golf](https://artigram.cafeboon.com/p/dh7qraw) - `Puzzle` `Physics` `Casual` - Slingshot a probe past twelve solar systems.
- [Higher Lower](https://playhigherlower.com/play) - `Trivia` `Estimation` - Choose a topic and guess which item has the higher value.
- [Ink Side Down](https://arkai.win/games/ink-side-down/) - `Puzzle` `Logic` `Daily` - Roll a cube whose inked side prints the square beneath it.
- [LemmingsJS](https://lemmingsjs.oklemenz.de/) - `Puzzle` `Strategy` `Real-Time` - Guide lemmings through obstacles to reach an exit.
- [Lie of Sight](https://lieofsight.com/) - `Puzzle` `Logic` `Daily` - Each say what they saw in a room, and one of them is lying.
- [Meowdoku](https://playmeowdoku.com) - `Puzzle` `Logic` - Blends Sudoku-style placement with Minesweeper-like deduction.
- [Morningwit Daily](https://morningwit.com/daily/) - `Puzzle` `Logic` `Daily` - One Binairo or Star Battle grid a day, same for everyone, layered hints.
- [One Step Late](https://arkai.win/games/one-step-late/) - `Puzzle` `Logic` `Daily` - Your shadow repeats each move one turn late; bring both of you home.
- [OpenTTD](https://pelya.github.io/openttd-touch-webapp/openttd.html) - `Tycoon` `Transportation` - Transporting passengers, minerals and goods via road, rail, water and air.
- [Pixel Dungeon](https://gnojus.github.io/pixel-dungeon-gdx/) - `Roguelike` `Turn-Based` `Dungeon crawler` `RPG` - Explore dungeon, collect items, fight monsters.
- [Rarest Answer](https://rarestanswer.com/?ref=awesome-browser-games) - `Trivia` `Daily` - Name the correct answer nobody else gives; rarer answers score more.
- [Saltwind](https://goblinarcade.com/play/saltwind) - `Trading` `Strategy` `Simulation` - Sail between ports: buy low, sell high and refit your ship.
- [Scalar](https://www.scalargame.com/scalar) - `Puzzle` `Geography` `Daily` - Deduce the mystery country from distance, area and population clues.
- [Tectonic](https://www.scalargame.com/tectonic) - `Puzzle` `Geography` `Daily` - Place each country beside its neighbours to build a 3D globe.
- [Thornreach](https://goblinarcade.com/play/thornreach) - `Strategy` `Real-Time` `Medieval` - Rise from landless knight to ruler: recruit, battle and siege.
- [Whenabouts](https://www.scalargame.com/whenabouts) - `Trivia` `History` `Daily` - Place five historical events on the year you think each happened.
- [WordleLike](https://wordlelike.com/) - `Puzzle` `Word` `Casual` - Daily games: Wordle, Word Hurdle, Mini Crossword, Word Search, Anagram.

## Multiplayer

- [Feudal Wars](http://feudalwars.net/) - `Strategy` `Real-Time` `Medieval` - Inspired by classic real-time strategy games.
- [Kings Cup Online](https://www.onlinekingscup.com/) - `Card` `Party` `Local` - Classic game on shared device, 5 rule sets incl. an alcohol-free mode.
- [Lagario 3](https://lagario.thomasar.dev) - `Action` `Real-Time` `Space` `P2P` - Slingshot your planet past stars with gravity and ram rivals.
- [Noillirk](https://noillirk.pro/) - `Party` `Word` `Real-Time` - Guess the most common answer to a prompt; live rooms with friends.
- [Parlour](https://parlour.cards) - `Card` `P2P` - Browser card table, Blitz and Wild, room codes.
- [Pokemon Arena 2D](https://poke2darena.com/) - `Action` `Real-Time` `Fan game` - Fan-made 2D Pokemon arena: gym bosses and online 1v1.
- [WebLiero](https://www.webliero.com/) - `Action` `Real-Time` - Battle worms armed with wide arsenal of weapons.
